01Some thoughts

My repository with deployment Django on Google Cloud Platform [GCP] was published in PyCoders weekly #532 and in Django Newsletter #135. How awesome is that! :)

I also added infra described in Terraform for setting up Open ID Connect authorization for GitHub Actions and GCP to my repository dedicated to this topic.

The talk Making Badass Developers and blog post The Ferrari problem made me realize how important it is to minimize the impact of not important or less important things in our lives. We should not burn our limited brain capacity to make some trivial choices like choosing t-shirt to wear today. This is of course a very trivial example but goes to the point of scarcity of computing power. Apparently I already got rid of the t-shirt problem since 98% of my t-shirts are plain black -> no choice -> no problem.

The first time Patrick Debois came into contact with Open Source was in the early stages of development of the Linux kernel, compiling it on floppies on his 486 machine.
